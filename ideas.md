<text>
**Design Movement:** Editorial Minimalism (inspired by high-end print journalism)

**Core Principles:**
- Stark contrast between large display type and fine body text
- Generous white space as a primary design element
- Content-first hierarchy — no decorative noise
- Precision in every typographic decision

**Color Philosophy:**
- Off-white (#F8F5F0) background evoking quality paper
- Near-black (#1A1A1A) for primary text and headings
- A single warm amber (#C9873A) as the sole accent — used sparingly for links, highlights, and dividers
- Emotional intent: authority, trust, and craft

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric editorial grid: large left-anchored headlines, right-column body text
- Horizontal rule dividers between sections, mimicking magazine layout
- Wide-margin hero with a large typographic number or stat as a visual anchor
- No hero image — typography IS the hero

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) in light weight as background texture
- Thin horizontal rules with amber accent dots at intersections
- Pull-quote blocks with oversized quotation marks

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal amber underlines on links with a slow fade
- Scroll-triggered section reveals — content slides up with opacity fade
- No flashy animations; motion is subtle and purposeful

**Animation:**
- Staggered fade-up on section entry (0.4s ease-out)
- Smooth scroll with offset for nav anchors
- Amber accent line draws in on section header hover

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (bold, italic for emphasis)
- Body: Source Serif 4 (regular, 18px, 1.8 line-height)
- Labels/Nav: DM Mono (uppercase, tracked)
</text>
</response>

<text>
**Design Movement:** Dark Executive — Corporate Noir

**Core Principles:**
- Dark, high-contrast surfaces that communicate authority and expertise
- Structured data presentation (credentials, stats) as design features
- Restrained use of a single vivid accent against deep neutrals
- Deliberate asymmetry to avoid corporate blandness

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep charcoal (#0F1117) background
- Warm off-white (#E8E4DC) for primary text
- Electric teal (#00C9A7) as accent — communicates precision and forward-thinking
- Subtle dark card surfaces (#1C1F2A) for section differentiation
- Emotional intent: expertise, modernity, confidence

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width hero with left-aligned text and a diagonal accent line cutting across
- Alternating left/right content blocks for experience and services
- Stats/credentials displayed in a horizontal band with large numerals
- Sticky side-nav for desktop, hamburger for mobile

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal teal accent lines as section dividers
- Credential badges rendered as styled "chips" with teal borders
- Large background numerals (faint) behind section headings

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Teal glow on card hover (box-shadow pulse)
- Navigation items underline with teal on hover
- CTA button fills with teal on hover with slight scale

**Animation:**
- Hero text types in character by character (typewriter effect)
- Cards slide in from left/right on scroll
- Teal accent line animates width on section entry

**Typography System:**
- Display: Space Grotesk (bold, 700)
- Body: Inter (400, 16px)
- Mono accents: JetBrains Mono for stats/labels
</text>
</response>

<text>
**Design Movement:** Refined Brutalism meets Professional Warmth

**Core Principles:**
- Strong typographic hierarchy with deliberate contrast between weights
- Warm neutral palette that feels human, not corporate
- Structural grid with intentional breaks for visual interest
- Content blocks that feel like they were composed, not generated

**Color Philosophy:**
- Warm cream (#FAF7F2) base — approachable yet refined
- Deep forest green (#1B3A2D) as primary dark — communicates trust, growth, expertise
- Muted gold (#B8924A) as accent — authority and premium quality
- Light sage (#E8EDE6) for alternating section backgrounds
- Emotional intent: trustworthy, expert, grounded, human

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Left-heavy hero: large name/title left-anchored, credentials float right
- Horizontal scrolling credential band (marquee) below hero
- Services in a 2-column asymmetric card grid
- Experience timeline on a vertical left-border line
- Full-width dark green CTA section near footer

**Signature Elements:**
- Thick left-border accent lines on section headings (gold)
- Credential/trust signal pill badges in sage green
- Oversized background text (faint forest green) behind key sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards lift with a subtle shadow on hover
- Nav links have a gold underline that slides in from left
- CTA button has a fill-from-left animation on hover

**Animation:**
- Framer Motion: fade-up with stagger on section entry
- Smooth anchor scroll
- Stats counter animation on scroll into view

**Typography System:**
- Display: Fraunces (variable, bold italic for hero)
- Body: Lato (400/700, 17px, 1.75 line-height)
- Labels/Nav: DM Sans (500, uppercase tracked)
</text>
</response>
